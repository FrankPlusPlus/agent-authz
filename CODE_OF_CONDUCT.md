# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

Participants must:

- communicate constructively and assume good faith;
- welcome different backgrounds and technical perspectives;
- focus feedback on ideas and behavior, not people;
- protect private or security-sensitive information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal
attacks, and publishing private information without consent. Maintainers may
remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the
community. Report concerns privately through the security or maintainer
contact listed by the repository.
